## 6 · Strict copywriting rules (every claim must trace to brief)

For v5, apply these rules to every line of copy:

1. **Verbatim or 1:1 synonym.** Every privilege name, every event description, every price number must appear in the brief or be a 1:1 synonym. Examples:
   - "именной халат" → ✓ verbatim; "персональный банный комплект" → ✗ invented.
   - "персональный администратор, который его ведет" → ✓; "лицо, сопровождающее вас" → ✗.
   - "ужин с основателем 2 раза в год для небольших групп" → ✓; "камерные ужины с основателем в узком кругу" → ✗ (paraphrase loses "небольших групп").
   - "закрытый чат в телеграмме" → ✓; "приватный мессенджер-канал" → ✗.

2. **No invented numbers.** If a number isn't in the brief, don't write it. Brief gives: 50, 50 000, 10 000, 40 000, 3, 2, 1. **Nothing else.** Drop: 42, 8, 7, 6 cities, 24 hours, 48 hours, 11th month, etc.

3. **No invented proper nouns.** Brief mentions: Woloshin Club, Woloshin Banya. **Nothing else.** Drop: "Rareș", "Издание I" (decision call — keep as light mark only), "Дом у леса", room names (Большая парная, Дубовая купель, etc.).

4. **No invented service descriptions.** Brief says "10% скидка на все комплексы и услуги" — period. Don't expand to "программы, кухни, любые услуги дома" (IN11). Don't invent "48 часов до публичного расписания" (IN12). Don't fabricate "не сгорает" (IN15).

5. **No invented event titles.** Brief says "3 мероприятия в год" — three. Don't name them. Don't give them dates. Don't write descriptions.

6. **No invented framing.** Brief says "ужин с основателем" — that's it. Don't make him a portrait (IN9), don't give him a first-person greeting, don't draw his signature. If founder context is needed, the brief already provides it: "основатель" appears once, as the host of the dinner.

7. **Decorative chrome is OK.** Three.js fog, grain texture, gold radial gradients, scroll progress hairline, ring counter SVG pattern (with brief's numbers 50/3/2) — keep. These don't make factual claims.

8. **Where v4 copy is closer to brief than the original agent drafts, keep v4.** Where v4 invents, replace with brief-exact text.
